# Remote and Hybrid Working Policy

Brightwater Solutions supports hybrid working. Most roles may work from home for up to three days per week, with at least two days in the Cork office. Tuesday is the company-wide office day, when team meetings and training take place.

## Eligibility
Hybrid working is available once you have completed your first month. Some roles, such as reception and facilities, must be fully office-based. Your manager will confirm your working pattern in writing.

## Your home workspace
You must have a safe, quiet place to work and a reliable internet connection. You must complete the home workstation self-assessment (form HS-HOME-01) before you start working from home. The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard and headset. You can claim a home working allowance of €3.20 per day for days worked from home.

## Security
Always connect through the company VPN, lock your screen when you step away, and never let family members use your work laptop. Confidential documents must not be printed at home.

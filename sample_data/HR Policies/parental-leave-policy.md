# Maternity, Paternity and Parental Leave Policy

Brightwater Solutions supports employees who are starting or growing a family.

## Maternity leave
Employees are entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave and may take up to 16 additional unpaid weeks. Brightwater tops up State Maternity Benefit to full salary for the first 18 weeks.

## Paternity leave
Fathers and second parents are entitled to 2 weeks of paternity leave, which must be taken within the first 6 months after the birth or adoption. Brightwater pays full salary during paternity leave.

## Parent's leave and parental leave
Each parent is also entitled to 9 weeks of paid parent's leave (paid by the State) in the first two years, and 26 weeks of unpaid parental leave before the child turns 12.

## How to apply
Tell HR at least 4 weeks before you want your leave to start, using the Family Leave form on the HR Portal.

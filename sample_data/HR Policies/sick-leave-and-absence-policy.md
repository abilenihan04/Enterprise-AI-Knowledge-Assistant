# Sick Leave and Absence Policy

If you are unwell and unable to work, you must phone your line manager before 9:30am on the first day of absence. A text message or email is not enough unless your manager has agreed to it. Tell your manager how long you expect to be absent.

## Certificates
For absences of up to two consecutive days you can self-certify by completing form HR-ABS-02 on the HR Portal when you return. Absences of three days or more require a medical certificate from a doctor.

## Sick pay
Brightwater pays full salary for up to 10 days of certified sick leave per year after you complete your probation period. After that, you may be entitled to Illness Benefit from the Department of Social Protection.

## Returning to work
After any absence longer than five days your manager will hold a short return-to-work meeting. If you have had repeated short absences, HR may refer you to the Occupational Health service to see what support we can offer.

# Annual Leave Policy

All full-time employees of Brightwater Solutions are entitled to 23 days of paid annual leave per year, plus Irish public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to the hours they work. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## Requesting leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance for periods of up to five days, and at least four weeks in advance for longer periods. Your line manager will approve or decline the request within five working days. Do not book flights or accommodation until your request has been approved.

## Busy periods
Leave is restricted during the last two weeks of the financial year (March) because of year-end reporting. Requests during this time are approved only in exceptional circumstances.

## Carrying over leave
Up to five unused days may be carried over into the first three months of the next leave year. Any days not used by 31 March are lost. Leave cannot be exchanged for payment except when an employee leaves the company.

## Contact
Questions about your leave balance should be sent to hr@brightwater.example.

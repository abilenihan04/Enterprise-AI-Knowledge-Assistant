# New Starter Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Brightwater Solutions. This guide explains what happens during your first week.

## Your first day
Please arrive at the Cork office reception at 9:30am and bring photo ID and your bank details for payroll. Your manager will meet you, give you a tour of the building and introduce you to your team. IT will give you your laptop, building access card and login details. You will have lunch with your team, paid for by the company.

## Your first week
During your first week you will complete mandatory online training on data protection (GDPR), information security and health and safety. You will be assigned a buddy, an experienced colleague who can answer everyday questions.

## Probation
All new employees have a six-month probation period. You will have review meetings with your manager at one month, three months and six months.

# Purchase Order Process

A purchase order (PO) must be raised before ordering any goods or services from a supplier, including software subscriptions and consultancy. Invoices without a PO number will not be paid.

## Raising a PO
Create a purchase requisition in the Finance Portal, attach the supplier's quote and select the budget code. The requisition is turned into a PO once it is approved, and the PO number is sent to the supplier.

## Approval limits
- Up to €1,000: line manager approval.
- €1,000 to €5,000: department head approval.
- Over €5,000: approval by the Finance Director, and three written quotes are required.
- Over €25,000: a formal tender process is required.

## New suppliers
New suppliers must complete the supplier set-up form and provide their tax and bank details. The finance team confirms bank details by phone before any payment is made.

# Business Travel Booking Policy

All business travel must be approved by your manager before anything is booked.

## Booking
Flights, trains and hotels are booked through the company's travel agent, TravelCo, using the travel request form in the Finance Portal. TravelCo will send options within one working day. Do not book travel yourself and claim it back, unless it is an emergency.

## Standards
- Economy class for flights under six hours.
- Hotels up to €180 per night in Europe and €250 per night in London, New York and other major cities.
- Book at least 14 days in advance where possible to keep costs down.

## Travel insurance and safety
Employees are covered by the company's travel insurance policy. Check the travel advice from the Department of Foreign Affairs before you go, and keep your manager informed of any changes to your plans.

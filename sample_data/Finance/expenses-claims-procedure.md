# Expenses Claims Procedure

Employees can claim back reasonable costs they pay personally while doing company business, such as client meals, taxis and conference fees.

## How to claim
Submit claims in the Expensify app within 30 days of the expense. Take a photo of each itemised receipt; a card payment slip is not enough. Claims are approved by your line manager and paid with your next monthly salary.

## Limits
- Meals with clients: up to €60 per person, including drinks. Write the names of the attendees and the business purpose on the claim.
- Meals while travelling: up to €35 per day.
- Taxis: allowed when public transport is not practical or after 9pm.
- Mileage for using your own car: paid at the civil service rates.

Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it is part of a client meal. Claims without receipts, or submitted more than 90 days late, will be rejected.

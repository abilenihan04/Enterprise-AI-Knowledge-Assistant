# Car Parking and Cycling Policy

The Cork office has 60 car parking spaces for about 200 staff, so spaces are shared.

## Parking permits
Staff who need to drive can apply for a parking permit from Facilities. Permits are given first to employees with a disability, car-sharers and staff who visit clients. Display your permit on the dashboard at all times. Cars without a permit may be clamped.

## Visitors
Five visitor spaces beside reception can be booked through Facilities at least one day in advance.

## Electric vehicles
Four spaces have electric vehicle chargers. Please move your car once it is fully charged.

## Cycling and public transport
There is secure bicycle storage behind the building, with showers and lockers on the ground floor. The company offers the Cycle to Work scheme and a tax-saver annual bus and train ticket, paid for through your salary.

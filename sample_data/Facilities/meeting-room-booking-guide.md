# Meeting Room Booking Guide

The Cork office has eight meeting rooms, from two-person focus rooms to the Atlantic boardroom, which holds 20 people.

## Booking a room
Book rooms through Microsoft Outlook: create a meeting, click "Room Finder" and choose a free room. Rooms can be booked up to three months ahead. The Atlantic boardroom must be booked through reception because it is often used for client visits.

## Equipment
All rooms have a screen with a wireless presentation connector and a Microsoft Teams camera and microphone. Ask Facilities at least two days in advance if you need catering.

## Good practice
Cancel your booking if you no longer need the room. Bookings that nobody checks in to within 15 minutes are released automatically. Leave the room tidy and wipe the whiteboard when you finish.

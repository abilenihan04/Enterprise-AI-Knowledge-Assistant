# Password Reset and Account Lockout Guide

Your Brightwater account is used for email, the HR Portal and all internal systems. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are changed every 180 days.

## If you have forgotten your password
Go to https://reset.brightwater.example from any device and sign in with your work email address. You will be asked to approve a notification in the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone. You can then choose a new password.

## If your account is locked
Your account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. If you need access sooner, phone the IT Service Desk on extension 4400 between 8am and 6pm.

## Multi-factor authentication
All accounts use multi-factor authentication. If you get a new phone, contact the Service Desk before you wipe your old phone so that the Authenticator app can be moved.

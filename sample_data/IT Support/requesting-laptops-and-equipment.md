# Requesting a Laptop, Monitor or Other IT Equipment

All IT equipment is ordered through the IT Service Portal so that it is set up securely and recorded in the asset register.

## Broken or faulty equipment
If your laptop is damaged or stops working, log an "Equipment fault" ticket. A replacement loan laptop can usually be collected from the IT desk on the second floor on the same day. Laptops are replaced every four years as standard.

## New equipment
To request extra equipment, such as a second monitor, docking station or headset, log an "Equipment request" ticket. Your manager must approve requests that cost more than €250. Standard items are delivered within five working days.

## Software
Only software from the Company Portal app may be installed. Requests for other software must be approved by IT Security.

## Leaving the company
All equipment must be returned to IT on your last day.

# Reporting Phishing and Suspicious Emails

Phishing emails try to trick you into clicking a link, opening an attachment or giving away passwords or bank details. They often look like they come from a manager, a supplier or a delivery company and try to create a sense of urgency.

## Warning signs
- The sender's address does not match the company it claims to be from.
- You are asked to pay an invoice urgently or change a supplier's bank details.
- The message asks you to sign in through a link to "keep your account active".

## What to do
Do not click any links or open attachments. Use the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook, which sends the email to the security team and removes it from your inbox. If you have already clicked a link or entered your password, phone the IT Service Desk on extension 4400 immediately and change your password.

The finance team will never ask you to change bank details by email alone. Always confirm by phoning the supplier on a number you already know.

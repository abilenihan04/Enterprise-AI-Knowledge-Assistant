# VPN Remote Access Guide

The Brightwater VPN (GlobalProtect) gives you a secure connection to internal systems when you are working away from the office.

## Connecting
1. Click the GlobalProtect globe icon in the system tray.
2. Enter the portal address vpn.brightwater.example.
3. Sign in with your work account and approve the Authenticator notification.
The icon turns blue with a shield when you are connected.

## Common problems
- Error 809 or "gateway not responding": your home router may be blocking the connection. Restart the router and try again, or connect using your phone's hotspot.
- Slow connection: disconnect from the VPN when you are using video calls in Microsoft Teams, which work without it.
- Repeated sign-in prompts: make sure the date and time on your laptop are correct.

If the problem continues, log a ticket in the IT Service Portal or phone extension 4400.

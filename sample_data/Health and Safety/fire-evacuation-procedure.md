# Fire Evacuation Procedure

## If you discover a fire
Raise the alarm by breaking the glass at the nearest fire alarm call point and phone 112. Only try to put out a small fire if you have been trained and it is safe to do so.

## When the alarm sounds
The fire alarm is a continuous loud bell. Leave the building immediately by the nearest exit, following the green signs. Do not use the lifts and do not stop to collect belongings. Close doors behind you.

## Assembly point
Go to the assembly point in the main car park beside the entrance gate. Fire wardens, who wear yellow vests, will check that everyone is accounted for. Do not go back into the building until the fire officer says it is safe.

## Testing
The alarm is tested every Wednesday at 11am; this test lasts less than a minute and you do not need to leave. A full evacuation drill takes place twice a year.

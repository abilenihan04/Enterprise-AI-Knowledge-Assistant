# Workplace Accident and Incident Reporting

Every accident, injury or near miss at work must be reported, even if no one was hurt.

## Immediately after an accident
Make sure the area is safe and get help from a trained first aider; their names are on the noticeboard in each kitchen. First aid kits are in every kitchen and at reception. For serious injuries phone 112.

## Reporting
Complete the incident report form in the Health and Safety section of the HR Portal within 24 hours. Describe what happened, where, who was involved and any witnesses. Your manager and the Health and Safety Officer will review the report.

## Serious incidents
Accidents that stop an employee from working for more than three days must be reported by the Health and Safety Officer to the Health and Safety Authority (HSA).

## Preventing accidents
Report hazards such as wet floors, trailing cables or broken chairs to Facilities straight away.
